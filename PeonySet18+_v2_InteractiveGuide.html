How PeonySet works: PeonySet is modular. The core systems form the base; optional modules can be enabled or disabled depending on the kind of RP you want. For the full interface experience, use the preset together with its Regex Pack.
🌸 What is PeonySet?

A roleplay world that keeps moving.

PeonySet was built around realism, character autonomy, hidden psychology, relationship progression and everyday world simulation. NPCs have their own goals, memories and reactions. Events can develop outside the user's immediate scene, while {{user}} remains under the user's control.

1. Import PeonySet 18+ v2 — English Preset into Tavo.
2. Enable the preset.
3. Import the accompanying Regex Pack.
4. Enable the Regex rules for the interface systems you want to use.
